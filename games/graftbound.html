    How to play GRAFTBOUND

    Move, attack and cast spells on the battlefield, then choose one card from each booster. Creature cards become grafts that change your stats, keywords and body. Rewards are real Magic: The Gathering cards adapted to GRAFTBOUND's own tactical rules.

    The Ranked Expedition uses one fixed seed and challenge for every player. Sign in to your Community profile, press START VERIFIED RUN, and play inside the game window. If your connection drops, resume from the last move the server accepted.
