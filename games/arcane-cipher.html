How to play

  1. Type any five-letter word using the keyboard on screen or your own keyboard, then press Enter.
  2. Forest-green letters are in the correct position. Tarnished-gold letters belong in the answer but in a different position. Slate letters are absent.
  3. You have six guesses. Repeat letters are scored carefully; every daily player receives the same answer.

Investigator's note

The answer is always a five-letter Magic-related word. Any five-letter A–Z guess is accepted so you never need to know a hidden dictionary.

How daily puzzles work

After the final guess, reveal the word's meaning and a related card on Scryfall. Daily progress is saved for signed-in investigators and locally for anonymous players.

Daily editions use curated Magic terms and real Magic cards. New puzzles follow midnight in America/Chicago, including daylight saving time. The selection is fixed per date for everyone and does not depend on their score or profile. No purchase or account required to play.
