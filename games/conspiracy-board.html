How to play

  1. Study the sixteen card names. Every name belongs to exactly one of four hand-checked groups.
  2. Tap four cards, then submit your group. Correct sets move to the solved record with a clear explanation.
  3. Four incorrect guesses end the case. A one-away message means three cards belong to the same unsolved group.
