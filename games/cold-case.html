  1. Read the first clue and select your suspect.

About Commander clues

All thirty launch commanders use original gameplay clues based on their Magic card identities and strategies. After you finish, the solution links to Scryfall and EDHREC so you can explore the commander for real.

What is Cold Case?

Cold Case is a free daily Magic: The Gathering guessing game that asks you to identify a legendary Commander creature by reading clues. Unlike a generic MTG card Wordle, each Cold Case focuses on a commander and its actual deckbuilding identity.

Can I play without an account?

Yes. Anyone can play the daily MTG game and unlimited practice cases. A free investigator account is only necessary to save long-term stats, join the daily leaderboard, and collect profile achievements.

Magic: The Gathering artwork and card links are supplied by Scryfall. Popularity numbers come from EDHREC, with a fixed, documented data snapshot.
