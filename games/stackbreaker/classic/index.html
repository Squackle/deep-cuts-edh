How to play the Classic campaign

Use MAP or the level selector to visit any stage. Choose from your seven-card hand, tap lands for mana, drag a spell onto its legal target or use keyboard controls, then PASS / RESOLVE to work through the stack. RESET restarts the current puzzle; UNDO reverses your last move.

Your completed stages, best stars, selected level, and local mastery rewards save in this browser. You can replay every stage. Classic is unranked; the Daily Stackbreaker challenge has separate, server-verified scoring. Saves on the standalone Game Lab's domain do not automatically transfer to this domain.
