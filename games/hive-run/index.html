PATHS OF THE HIVE.

Predator Hunt turns your body into a trap. Burrow Stalker and Tunnel Weaver need two captures; Hatchery Warden and Rift Guardian need three. Brood Trail follows the classic Snake rhythm: collect an orb, grow, repeat. Both paths descend through the Hatchery, Burrows and Fracture.

Daily Ritual uses a shared seed and resets at midnight Central. Retry freely; each completed run can be submitted. Rankings are separate for every path and expedition.

PATHS OF THE HIVE1.0 / TWO MODES · FOUR DEPTHS

Distinct bosses, living terrain, daily seeded challenges and separate records. Slower opening movement gives you time to plan. Music builds from a quiet Hatchery to the deepest Fracture.

FIELD GUIDE · ENCOUNTERS & MUTATIONS

Evolution 2: Burrow Stalker relocates five tiles after a warning. Evolution 4: Hatchery Warden seals up to three tiles. Evolution 6: Tunnel Weaver places webs that briefly freeze the hunt. Evolution 8: Rift Guardian warns before rupturing nearby tiles.

Boss powers charge for ten active seconds. The ochre ring closes when ready. The field pauses between capture phases. Only the final capture grants a reward. Brood Trail has no encounters.

Areas change every four evolutions. Reach twelve for the Deep Fracture. Expeditions finish after 6,000 simulation steps, or earlier if your swarm crashes. Shields and molts can save you; the walls and your own body remain dangerous.
