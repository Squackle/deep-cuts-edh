    FIELD GUIDE · HOW TO PLAY & ENCOUNTERS

    PREDATOR HUNT: Trap prey with your living body. Your first ordinary-prey collision is forgiven; early enemies move more slowly. Minibosses take two captures and full bosses take three.

    BROOD TRAIL: Classic Snake. Swipe or use arrow keys / WASD, collect brood orbs and grow the swarm. No bosses or enemies.

    EVOLUTIONS: Captures earn 3 energy; Brood Trail orbs earn 1. At 5 energy, pick an adaptation: white shields, blue slows, black sheds the tail, red boosts speed and points, green grants growth.

    ENCOUNTERS: Evolution 2: Burrow Stalker moves five tiles after a warning. Evolution 4: Hatchery Warden seals tiles. Evolution 6: Tunnel Weaver lays webs that stun. Evolution 8: Rift Guardian creates ruptures. Their powers charge for 10 active seconds, indicated by an ochre ring.

    ENVIRONMENTS: The background changes after evolutions 4, 8 and 12. Dashed tiles warn before hazards become solid. Boss briefings pause the game, then begin with a three-second countdown.

    EXPEDITIONS: Arcade is the permanent ranked competition with a fresh seed each run. Predator Hunt also offers unranked boss practice. Signed-in Field Network profiles can submit server-verified Arcade scores.
