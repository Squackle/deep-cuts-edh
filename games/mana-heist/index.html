The case file

The current ward requires the mana shown in its cost.

How it works

Play lands from your hand onto the battlefield by dragging or selecting them. Tap a land or mana artifact to use its available mana ability. Some sources produce several mana at once; others require spending mana, paying life, or sacrificing themselves first. Restricted mana can only pay for permitted spells. Mana enters the cauldron as separate colors, even when the liquid blends visually. Cast Spell spends legal mana; optional auto-cast handles a valid payment for you. The current campaign uses a simplified turn structure: floating mana clears when you end your turn, while actual Magic clears it at the end of every step and phase. Undo or restart any vault. Keyboard: Tab to a card; use Left/Right or Up/Down arrows to move between cards in the same rack, Home/End for the first/last card, Enter or Space to use it, I to inspect it, and Escape to dismiss choices.

Three-star route

The solver scores the fewest turns and least life lost, not the number of taps. In this simplified puzzle, mana empties at the end of your turn (in real Magic it empties at the end of each step or phase). Numerals are generic costs payable with any mana; a diamond requires true colorless mana. Future chapters unlock sequentially.
