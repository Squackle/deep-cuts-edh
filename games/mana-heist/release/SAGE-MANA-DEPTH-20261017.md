# Mana Heist — Sage / Mana Depth Lab Release (2026-10-17)

**Owner authorization:** “go ahead and deploy this,” referring to the four-part plan: tapped-card correction, 18 bespoke lesson hints + compact results, mana beyond lands including Dark Ritual and Sol Ring, and a genuinely stronger late-game difficulty curve. This applies to the standalone **Game Lab** only. Public Deep Cuts EDH promotion remains BLOCKED under rights gate P01 and requires its own approval.

## Delivered in the existing Mana Heist game

**Card and results:** Corrected legacy `inset:auto` override that was displacing a sideways card image outside its landscape hitbox. Full Magic card frame rotates correctly. Hover magnifier is suppressed after a completed stage; no giant black tooltip remains over the result. Completion panel is now a compact centered overlay with a useful explanation, Next and Return actions, bounded height and keyboard/touch support. Original Chromatic Cauldron, orb-flight, main fixed-screen gameplay and non-scrolling behavior remain.

**Learn Mana and the Sage:** All original 18 authored exercises retain IDs and order; each has a distinct, state-aware **The Sage’s Notes** hint relevant to its actual board. Lesson 5 explicitly teaches blue + red mixing, while distinguishing *visual liquid blending* from individual mana units. Hints are positioned to the LEFT of the pot on desktop, in a small collapsible card on phone. An optional **Sage’s Whisper** appears after two restarts in a challenge, tagged to a relevant skill/lesson. New lesson-only exercises 19–26 add Sol Ring, Azorius Signet, Dark Ritual, a paid-filter mana pipeline, Pyretic Ritual, Ancient Ziggurat, Urborg/Cabal Coffers and Lotus Field. These eight new boards are solver-proven and replay-tested. Learn completions remain under `dc-mana-heist-learn-v1`; they award **no puzzle medals**.

**Mana rule extension:** Dark Ritual is a cast **instant**, not a land or an activated mana ability: pay B, produce BBB, move the spell from hand to the graveyard. Pyretic Ritual costs {1}{R}, makes RRR, and also moves to graveyard. Existing Sol Ring, Signets, Coffers, Nykthos, Lotus Field, Treasure, Lotus Petal and filters keep their actual modeled roles. Supported scenario limit increases to 18 sources and 16 mana symbols in a ward. Generic and exact colored mana-unit payments use combinations rather than redundant factorial permutations; paid ability choices collapse equivalent color-allocation paths. No mana from unauthorized restricted source can pay a fee. These are deliberately **bounded puzzle mechanics**, not full Magic combat/priority/stack simulations. The main campaign STILL empties mana when End Turn is pressed, rather than implementing every official step/phase.

**30 revised late-game puzzles:** The last TWO vaults of each world in Galaxies III, IV and V now have dedicated 8–12-source strategic scenarios, with real activation-fee chains, ritual sequences, Urborg/Coffers scaling, Nykthos devotion, exact mana colors and one-shot artifact decisions. The final stage `mh-250` now asks for `{B}{B}{B}{B}{U}{U}{U}{U}{R}{R}{W}{W}{C}{2}`, requiring both paid filters and a ritual on a dense board. Every revised puzzle has a deterministic **verified legal-route certificate**; this proves solvability but **does not claim optimality or unique solution**. Its result copy explicitly describes the route as a reference route. The remaining 202 generated variants have NOT been comprehensively re-authored or difficulty-calibrated; full 250-level difficulty acceptance G2 remains IN PROGRESS / incomplete. This is a substantial first correction, not a false claim of 250 unique expert puzzles.

**Preserve saves:** All 250 stage IDs, the original 18 authored stages, old medals, and `dc-mana-heist-landfall-v2` remain. Because 30 stages changed, their NEW medals are stored separately in `dc-mana-heist-capstones-v1`, so old bests are not silently reinterpreted under new rules. The old records remain archived in the original guest save. Only Game Lab localStorage is used. No accounts, profile achievements, servers, leaderboards, D1, or public site.
