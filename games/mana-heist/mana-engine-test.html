Engine scope & rules references

Each mana unit is tracked individually. An ability requiring mana must be paid before its output is added; restricted mana is marked CREATURE ONLY. Mana disappears when you advance to another phase, and your permanents untap only when you enter your next turn. The phase track is deliberately abbreviated: actual Magic also has steps within phases.

These puzzles start with permanents already established on the battlefield. They do not simulate the earlier enter-the-battlefield triggers or land drops, and the engine is not a full Magic game. Some displayed exercises specify existing devotion from other permanents outside the test area.
