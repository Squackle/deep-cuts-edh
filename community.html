SUBMIT A DECKLIST

Sign in with your Community profile, paste a decklist snapshot, and optionally cite a public Moxfield, Archidekt, Deckstats or MTGGoldfish deck URL. We do not fetch external lists or alter your collection. The submitted snapshot becomes a public Deep Cuts challenge deck, preserving the existing vote and discussion system. Submitting again replaces your current entry for this challenge; old votes do not transfer to the replacement.
