Simple, free daily MTG games

Every day brings the same four puzzles for everyone. Cold Case offers five detective clues, Obscurity Ladder ranks Commander cards, Arcane Cipher challenges you to identify a five-letter creature type and Conspiracy Board hides four groups of related cards.

New daily puzzles arrive at midnight Central Time. Practice games are unlimited, but do not earn achievements.

Investigator achievements

Playing is free and anonymous. Sign in with a free Field Network profile if you want saved scores, daily rankings, completion records, and collectible badges on your public investigator dossier.

Scores can be shared without spoilers. Each daily Ladder is frozen to a dated source snapshot. New puzzles use Scryfall EDHREC card-usage ranks, which do not measure how many decks a card leads as commander.

About the Commander popularity data

Starting October 14, 2026, Obscurity Ladder uses Scryfall EDHREC card-use rankings, refreshed automatically and frozen per daily puzzle. Higher numeric rank means lower use as a card in EDHREC decklists. This is not a count of decks led by that commander, nor a measure of strength. The October 13 launch puzzle keeps its original manually recorded EDHREC commander deck-count snapshot. Both games use Scryfall for card artwork and identification links.
