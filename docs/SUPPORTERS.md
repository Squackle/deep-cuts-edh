# Ko-fi supporter memberships

The public Deep Cuts EDH supporter destination is **https://ko-fi.com/deepcutsedh**. Ko-fi is the chosen provider. Three tiers are proposed (Informant $5, Detective $10, Chief Investigator $25 per month); their Ko-fi publishing status has not been verified.

Payment processing requires the creator to connect their own PayPal or Stripe account; a PayPal **Business** account can display a business name and contact email instead of the legal name normally shown by a Personal account. Verify actual transaction-visible information before promoting payments.

**Important:** The Field Office's key-based premium access is *not yet connected to Ko-fi*. The Ko-fi webhook can securely record qualifying renewal payments after secret configuration, but Ko-fi does not send cancellation webhooks. Keys will require ownership verification and automatic expiration on nonrenewal before launch.

See [KO-FI.md](KO-FI.md) for the setup checklist and secure webhook instructions.
