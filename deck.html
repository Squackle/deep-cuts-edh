03 / FIELD DIAGNOSTICS

The Apothecary's Notes

Role estimates and probability calculations based on your resolved card list. Advisory, not a playtest or a certified bracket rating.

Analyze a deck to unlock the complete workshop report.

Mana curve

Nonland cards, including commanders when resolved. Mana value 7+ grouped.

Opening-hand odds

Exact hypergeometric calculations, on a 100-card deck assumption, without mulligans. Ramp probability counts cards classified as ramp, not whether their mana costs can be paid.

Deck roles

Oracle text heuristics; some cards fill multiple roles. Confirm edge cases manually.

Strategy / table feel

Experimental pattern flags, not ML win-turn, archenemy scoring, or combo-proof classification.
