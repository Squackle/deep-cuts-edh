RESCUE REPORT / 001MONO-BLACK REANIMATOR

THE DEEP CUTS FIELD GUIDE · PROTOTYPE EDITION

Chainer, Dementia Master:
Steal death itself.

Chainer can turn anyone's graveyard into your own creature collection. Here's how we turn a very old, very risky legend into a meaningful reanimation engine.

01 / The diagnosis

Chainer, Dementia Master is a five-mana black legendary creature with an expensive but potent ability: pay three black mana and three life, then put a creature card from a graveyard onto the battlefield under your control. The creature becomes a Nightmare. When Chainer leaves the battlefield, all Nightmares are exiled.

That last clause is the trap. You can spend turns assembling an incredible board, only to lose every stolen creature to one removal spell.

02 / The prescription

Rather than pretending the weakness doesn't exist, we build around it. Fill everyone's graveyards, make abundant black mana, and sacrifice Chainer's Nightmares for value before Chainer disappears. Sacrificed creatures enter a graveyard instead of being stranded on the battlefield when Chainer leaves, allowing later reanimation.

The cards that make it work

Viscera Seer and Carrion Feeder are inexpensive sacrifice outlets that can cash in Nightmares in response to removal. Buried Alive searches for threats and fills your graveyard. Gray Merchant of Asphodel helps stabilize your life total while pressuring every opponent.

03 / The game plan

Early: Establish mana rocks and put useful creatures into graveyards. Midgame: Resolve Chainer with enough black mana to activate him, ideally with a sacrifice outlet already in play. Late: Reanimate high-impact threats and sacrifice them when needed, rebuilding after removal rather than going all-in on one fragile board.

04 / The weaknesses

Graveyard hate, instant-speed commander removal, life-total pressure, and the triple-black activation cost can all shut the plan down. Save interaction for the threats that prevent the deck from functioning, not just the creatures that look biggest.

05 / The starter prescription

This is an incomplete sample core, deliberately not represented as a tested 100-card deck or a price-verified $100 list. Use it as a starting point for brewing and testing.

RoleCard

Our verdict

Rescue potential: promising, but untested. Chainer gives you a plan that really requires your commander. The aim isn't to build a generic black goodstuff deck; it's to play a game no other mono-black legend plays quite the same way.
